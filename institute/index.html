Nolan Institute of Global Business & Technology is a technology-enabled education and professional development institution focused on building the financial, technological, business and entrepreneurial capabilities required to thrive in the global economy.

Nolan Institute develops people, businesses and organisations through technology-enabled education in Business, Finance, Technology and Entrepreneurship.

Our programmes are practical, applied and designed around the real capability gaps that individuals and organisations face — from financial modelling and capital markets to data analytics, AI and digital transformation.

Core Platforms

Five capability areas, one integrated institution — built for individuals, businesses and organisations.

01 — Professional Education

Professional Education

Nolan Institute helps individuals develop practical skills for employment, career advancement and professional development.

Areas include:

  • Accounting & Finance
  • Banking & Financial Services
  • Investment & Capital Markets
  • Financial Modelling
  • Business Management
  • Project Management
  • Data Analytics
  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Digital Transformation
  • Entrepreneurship
  • Leadership
Delivery formats: These are delivered through short courses, certificates, professional programmes, bootcamps and advanced programmes.
02 — Executive & Management Education

Executive & Management Education

For managers, executives, business owners and professionals who need to develop higher-level capabilities and practical application.

Programmes include:

  • Executive Finance
  • Strategic Management
  • Digital Transformation
  • Business Strategy
  • Leadership
  • Corporate Finance
  • Risk Management
  • Innovation
  • Entrepreneurship
  • AI for Business
03 — Corporate Training & Workforce Development

Corporate Training & Workforce Development

Nolan Institute can work directly with organisations to identify capability gaps and design training programmes around their workforce.

Example 1: A bank needs stronger data capabilities.
Nolan designs and delivers a Data & AI for Financial Services Academy.
Example 2: An SME group needs stronger financial management.
Nolan delivers a Finance for Business Owners Programme.
04 — Technology & Digital Skills

Technology & Digital Skills

Technology is one of the defining pillars of the institution.

Programmes include:

  • Digital literacy
  • Data analytics
  • AI
  • Programming
  • Cybersecurity
  • Cloud computing
  • Digital product development
  • FinTech
  • Business intelligence
  • Digital transformation
The goal isn't necessarily to turn everyone into a software engineer. It's to help professionals and organisations use technology effectively.
